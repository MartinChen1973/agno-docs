# BingChuan Ice Cream Company

## Company Overview

BingChuan is a premium ice cream manufacturer founded in 2015, specializing in artisanal frozen desserts made with natural ingredients. The company operates production facilities in three regions and distributes products to over 500 retail locations nationwide.

## Product Line

### Signature Flavors

- **Classic Vanilla Bean**: Made with Madagascar vanilla extract and fresh cream
- **Dark Chocolate Fudge**: Rich Belgian chocolate with chocolate chunks
- **Strawberry Fields**: Fresh strawberries sourced from local farms
- **Salted Caramel**: Handcrafted caramel with sea salt crystals
- **Matcha Green Tea**: Premium Japanese matcha powder

### Seasonal Specials

BingChuan releases limited-edition flavors quarterly:

- Spring: Lavender Honey
- Summer: Mango Coconut
- Fall: Pumpkin Spice
- Winter: Peppermint Bark

## Manufacturing Process

All ice cream is produced using traditional methods:

1. **Ingredient Sourcing**: Premium ingredients sourced from certified suppliers
2. **Small Batch Production**: Batches limited to 50 gallons for quality control
3. **Slow Churning**: 20-minute churn time for optimal texture
4. **Flash Freezing**: Rapid freezing at -40°F to preserve flavor and texture
